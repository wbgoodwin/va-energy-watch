## What Dominion is asking for

Rider SNA is a separate line in Dominion's rates that pays for its **nuclear life extension program** at the Surry
(Units 1 and 2) and North Anna (Units 1 and 2) power stations. It covers two things:

- preparing applications to the Nuclear Regulatory Commission to renew the plants' operating licenses for another 20
  years, and
- upgrading or replacing equipment, such as cables, generators, condensers and fuel handling systems, so the plants can
  run safely in that extra time.

Each year Dominion files an update. This one asks for **$281,484,106** for the rate year from September 1, 2027 to
August 31, 2028. That total has two parts: $272,283,662 for projected costs during the rate year and $9,200,444 to
true up last year's actual costs (Order for Notice and Hearing, page 3; Teconchuk testimony summary).

Dominion uses its authorized return on equity in the calculation: 9.8% from November 25, 2025, set in its 2025
biennial review (PUR-2025-00058).

## What it means for your bill

Dominion says a typical home using 1,000 kWh a month would pay **about $0.35 more per month** than under the current
Rider SNA, starting September 1, 2027 (Powell testimony summary; Order for Notice and Hearing, page 3). That is the change
in this one rider, not your whole bill.

## The schedule

The SCC's Order for Notice and Hearing (November 24, 2026) assigned a Hearing Examiner and set the dates listed above.
SCC Staff will investigate and file its own testimony by March 23, 2027. Groups that want to formally take part must
file a notice of participation by January 13, 2027 and need a lawyer to do so.

## Background

The SCC approved Phase II of this program (2025 through 2027) in case PUR-2024-00154. Dominion says it has finished work
on 15 upgrade projects during scheduled outages, 10 at Surry and 5 at North Anna, and that contingency budgets have
absorbed cost increases so far (O'Brien testimony summary). Detailed cost documents were filed under seal as
extraordinarily sensitive, so they are not public.
